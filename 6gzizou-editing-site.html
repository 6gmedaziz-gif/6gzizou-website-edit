How I work · Preparation in weeks

From raw files to final export

  1. Start

    Brief

    You send the footage link, references and the platforms it is for. I confirm the scope and how many weeks each video needs.

  2. Week 1

    Rough cut

    A first full edit with structure, pacing and temporary music, so you can judge the story early.

  3. Week 2

    Revisions

    I take notes by timecode and revise until the cut is right.

  4. Final week

    Delivery

    Final MP4 (H.264) in each ratio you need, plus captions and ProRes if requested.

Note: The deadline of every video can change from one video to another. Each video has its own preparation time in weeks.

Pricing

How the price is set

I quote each video after I read your order. The price depends on three things.

  1. Time

    Length and preparation

    The length of the finished video and the number of weeks you give me to prepare it.

  2. Clips

    Number of clips

    The more clips I have to sort, cut and join, the more work the edit takes.

  3. Details

    What you want in it

    Effects, animation, subtitles, sound and color work. Write them in the details box for each video.
